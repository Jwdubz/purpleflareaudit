What I found

01

The homepage is very slow on phones.

The big video at the top is heavy. In my phone test the screen stayed mostly blank for a long time, and the page didn't respond to taps while it loaded. Many people won't wait that long. They'll call the next shop on the list.

02

“Get a Quote” often sends people back to the start.

On pages like Packages, Gallery, Reviews and About, the quote button jumps back to the homepage form. That means loading the slowest page again, right when someone is ready to ask for a price.

03

Several pages are missing a main headline for Google.

Gallery, Packages, Reviews, About and Blog have no main heading. Your FAQ answers are also set up in a way Google can't follow.

04

Small cleanups

Some headings are hard to read against the background, a few photos aren't described for screen readers, and your map is set to a foreign language.

What's working

Your phone number is tappable on every page, the service pages are detailed and helpful, and almost every service page has its own quote form.

Visitor numbers, rankings, and whether quote requests reach you. I didn't submit your forms or look at private data.
